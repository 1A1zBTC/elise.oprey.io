Pet Lovers

Your house is ready for pets — now go meet them! Visit the 🏥 vet clinic, meet the pets in the pen and adopt your first friend with your pocket money. Care for them, earn coins and adopt more! 💕

🚶 Walk with the arrows / WASD, or tap where to go
🐾 Stand next to a pet to feed, play, wash & clean
🎮 Every chore is a mini game — and every pet plays differently! Do well and it helps your pet lots more!
🛒 Walk to the shop counter to visit the PET STORE — tap what you want to buy: food, treats, gear, beds & more, then tap the register to check out! Chores need the right gear: a toy to play, 🧼 soap to wash, a 🧹 broom to clean and a 🦮 leash to walk!
🏥 Want a new pet? Visit the vet clinic and meet the pets! Pick your favourite, buy everything on their supply list at the store, then come back to buy your pet — and build their home piece by piece at their spot!
🧴 Keep the litter box filled and 🧻 pee pads fresh — or your cat & dog will pee on the floor and you'll have puddles to mop! And put 🏖️ sand & dirt in the hermit crab's tank or Sheldon hides in his shell!
🐕 Take the dog for walks — tap the bones!
🚑 Sick pet? Visit the vet clinic counter, pick your pet and press Go to the vet!
💢 Some pets don't get along — if a fight breaks out, run over and break it up fast, or someone gets hurt 🤕 and needs the vet!
📱 Your phone rings when a neighbour wants a dog walk — press P to answer! You have 10s to pick up and 5s to say yes or no
🧺 Open the phone's Foster app (press P) to choose a pet to foster — happy fosters pay more!

Happy pets drop hearts — walk over them to grow your ❤ Love! Use your job money for supplies and new pets! 🐷
