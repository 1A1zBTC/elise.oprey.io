Move: P1 = WASD, P2 = arrows (or drag)  ·  Sprint: hold Shift (tires you out!)  ·  Pass: R / 2  ·  Shoot (hold) / Tackle / Header: F / 4  ·  Switch: Q / 1  ·  Sub: E / 0

On phone: drag the pitch to steer the joystick 🕹️  ·  tap the buttons to pass, shoot, switch & sub.

Ball out the sides = throw-in  ·  out over a goal line = corner or goal kick, just like real life.
