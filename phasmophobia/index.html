Phasmophobia

You're a ghost hunter. 👻 A ghost is haunting this house — figure out which kind and get out alive.

Take equipment from your belt: the EMF reader 📟, spirit box 📻, thermometer 🌡️, UV light 🔦, ghost writing book 📖 and video camera 📹. Each ghost leaves exactly 3 kinds of evidence — find them, open your journal 📓, pick the ghost that matches, then walk back to the van and hit LEAVE.

Stay in the dark too long and your sanity drops. Low sanity means the ghost will hunt — if the lights start flashing, run and hide in a wardrobe (H) until it stops.
