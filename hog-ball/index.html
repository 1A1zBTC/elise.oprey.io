Move: P1 = WASD, P2 = arrows (or drag)  ·  Shoot (hold, release in the green at the top; a quick tap = pump fake 🎣) / jump-block / reach in: Q / 4  ·  Turbo: hold Shift / 5 💨  ·  E / 3: hold to auto-defend, tap with the ball for a crossover  ·  Pass: R / 2  ·  Switch: F / 1

GREEN = automatic bucket — the sliver shrinks when you're guarded, deep or gassed (turbo drains your legs!)  ·  free throws use the same timing bar — tap to stop it in the green (3 shots if fouled on a three, and-one if the bucket dropped!)  ·  3 straight makes = TAKEOVER 🔥 (bigger green + faster legs, a miss cools you off)  ·  5 team fouls a quarter = bonus  ·  dribble moves (E/3) can break ankles 😵  ·  1P starts with a jump ball — mash Space!
MY TEAM is its own mode: pull cards, spend 🪙 coins to upgrade a card's OVERALL, then win three knockout rounds to earn ten picks on the draft board.
Reaching in (Q/4) is a gamble — you throw a hand at the ball and the outcome lands half a second later, so a handler who pulls it back or crosses over beats you. Clean strips are rare; usually you knock it loose for a scramble. Whiff and you're off balance while they go past; reach from a bad angle and it's a foul. Quarters are 90 seconds with a 24-second shot clock — work the ball, crash the glass and take the good look.
