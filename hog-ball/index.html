My Lineup

Tap Start/Bench to build your three — any position anywhere. Sell pays 85% of value.

Move: P1 = WASD, P2 = arrows (or drag)  ·  Shoot (hold, release in the green at the top; a quick tap = pump fake 🎣) / jump-block / steal try: Q / 4  ·  Turbo: hold Shift / 5 💨  ·  E / 3: hold to auto-defend, tap with the ball for a crossover  ·  Pass: R / 2  ·  Switch: F / 1

GREEN = automatic bucket — the sliver shrinks when you're guarded, deep or gassed (turbo drains your legs!)  ·  free throws use the same timing bar — tap to stop it in the green (3 shots if fouled on a three, and-one if the bucket dropped!)  ·  3 straight makes = TAKEOVER 🔥 (bigger green + faster legs, a miss cools you off)  ·  5 team fouls a quarter = bonus  ·  dribble moves (E/3) can break ankles 😵  ·  1P starts with a jump ball — mash Space!
