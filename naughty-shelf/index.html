Naughty Shelf

More and more corgis wander in. When one growls, pees or poops, drag it up to the naughty shelf. But a corgi left on the shelf gets sad and cries — drag it back down!
You lose if the floor gets too naughty, or a shelf corgi gets too sad. 🐶
